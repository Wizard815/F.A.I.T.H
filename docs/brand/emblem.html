FAITH keeps the hive.

A d20 holds a small hive of low-poly bees, each tied to the others and to a glowing core by thread. Two gold rings turn around it. The bees are the family and the apps FAITH connects; the threads are how they reach each other; the rings keep watch.

The feel

Warm, not techy

Espresso and walnut browns at night, parchment by day. Gold is the one accent: buttons, the active tab, a person's own things.

Brass instruments

Thin gold hairlines, ring ticks like an astrolabe, small round beads. Cards are framed like brass-edged panels, not glass.

Hexagons for the hive

Face tiles, points and the core are hexagons. Threads link related things. FAITH's emblem sits where the agent is: the chat, the wall screen, voice.
