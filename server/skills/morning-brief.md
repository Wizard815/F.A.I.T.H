name: morning-brief
title: "Morning brief"
description: "A short good-morning summary of the day ahead, for a person or the whole family. Use it for \"what's my day look like\" in the morning and for the morning routine."
---
1. Get today's plan with allglance_get_day (with the person's owner_id when you're talking with one person).
2. Look at today's meals with meals, and the weather with home_find (domain weather) if it's available.
3. Check chores still to do with allglance_list_chores.
4. If anything is waiting for approval, mention how many (approvals are on the family's screens and in the parents' chat).

Write it like a friendly note on the fridge:
- Start with "Good morning" and the person's name (or "everyone").
- Times first, in order. Leave out anything that already happened.
- One line on the weather and one on dinner.
- Under 8 lines. No headings.
