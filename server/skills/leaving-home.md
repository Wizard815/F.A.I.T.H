title: "Leaving the house"
description: "A quick check before everyone leaves: doors, garage, lights and anything left on."
---
1. home_find with domain lock: list anything unlocked.
2. home_find with domain cover: anything open (garage, windows).
3. home_find with domain light, and media_player: anything still on.
4. Tell the person in one or two lines what's open or on, then offer to lock and switch off. Locks and covers need their Yes; lights you can switch off once they say so.
